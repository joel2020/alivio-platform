# Data Processing Addendum — DRAFT (requires attorney review before any client use)

Status: internal draft, 2026-07-22. Not to be sent to clients until reviewed by counsel.

## Parties and roles
Alivio Search Partners, Inc. ("Processor") processes candidate and hiring data on behalf of the client ("Controller") to deliver recruiting services under the master services agreement.

## Scope of processing
- Categories of data subjects: job candidates, client hiring contacts.
- Categories of data: identity and contact details, professional history, licensure/certifications, screening call recordings and transcripts (with disclosure/consent), interview feedback, compensation expectations.
- Purpose: sourcing, scoring, screening, shortlist delivery, and reporting for the Controller's open roles. No sale of personal data; no use for unrelated model training.

## Commitments (to mirror the published privacy policy and Responsible AI statement)
1. Process only on documented instructions from the Controller.
2. Confidentiality: personnel bound by confidentiality obligations.
3. Security: encryption in transit and at rest; organization-scoped access (row-level security); access on a need-to-know basis.
4. Subprocessors: the published list at aliviosearchpartners.com/privacy (Vercel, Supabase, Microsoft Azure OpenAI, Resend, Cal.com, Google Cloud Vertex AI, GitHub); Controller notified of changes.
5. Data subject requests: assist the Controller; direct-received requests forwarded within 7 days.
6. Breach notification: without undue delay after becoming aware, with known details.
7. AI processing: human review before candidates are advanced or rejected; automated screening calls disclose themselves; scores accompanied by written rationale.
8. Deletion/return: within 30 days of termination on written request, subject to legal retention obligations.
9. International transfers: [SCCs to be attached where applicable — ATTORNEY INPUT REQUIRED].
10. Audit: annual written attestation; audits on reasonable notice at Controller's cost.
